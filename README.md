# Story Loom

Story Loom writes a story with an AI model that runs on your own computer.
You say what should happen in a chapter; the model plans the chapter and
then writes it, one part at a time. Nothing is sent to or fetched from the internet.

> 🎬 **See it in action:** [Basic usage](docs/USAGE.md) — a video and a
> sketch of how a chapter goes from your idea to finished prose.

## How it works, in one minute

1. **You say what happens.** A few sentences of plot instructions for a
   chapter.
2. **The model plans.** It writes the chapter's plot, divided into parts,
   and you keep it or ask for another.
3. **The model writes.** One part at a time, each from its own plan and
   from everything written before it. Don't like a part? Ask for another
   version and choose the one that counts.
4. **You take it with you.** Download the finished story as a text or
   Markdown file.

## What you need

A Windows PC with an **NVIDIA graphics card with 24 GB** of memory, an
internet connection for the one-time downloads, and about 40 GB of free
disk space. No graphics card that size? Watch [Basic usage](docs/USAGE.md)
to see what it does.
