P1 TESTABLE seed: A thing being built toward is touched at least three times before it lands, in three different scenes; two touches read as repetition and one reads as set dressing.
P2 TESTABLE seed: Escalate groundwork by consequence, not by mention — each touch costs somebody something. Restating the same fact more loudly is not escalation.
P3 ADVISORY seed: Groundwork already listed for this story is authoritative; work toward it and let it arrive where the scene has room, never force it into one that does not.
P4 TESTABLE seed: Every exchange has a want on each side, and at least one of those wants goes unstated.
P5 TESTABLE seed: Nobody says the thing they mean first.
P6 TESTABLE seed: An exchange changes something — a decision, a balance of power, or what one of them knows. Talk that leaves the scene where it found it does not belong in it.
P7 TESTABLE seed: What speech establishes, the narration does not then repeat.
P8 TESTABLE seed: In any passage carrying something withheld, at least one behaviour, choice or omission is explained by it and by nothing else on the page. A withheld thing that changes nothing is not withheld; it is absent.
P9 TESTABLE seed: Someone who does not know shows a specific unexplained thing, not a vague unease.
P10 ADVISORY owner: A withheld thing is introduced into the story, never expired out of it. Paying it off is welcome; dissolving it is forbidden — it stays withheld even once characters and reader know.
P11 TESTABLE seed: A scene is a unit of change. If the change cannot be named, it is not a scene yet.
P12 ADVISORY seed: Stop mid-motion at the part's edge rather than closing it off.
P13 TESTABLE owner: Use the designation the established material gives; never widen a named thing to its category.
