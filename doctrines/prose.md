S1 TESTABLE owner: Render a moment, do not report it. A change of state given less than a paragraph has been summarised away, and the reader loses what actually happened and what it looked like.
S2 TESTABLE seed: What a moment costs in words is decided by what it is worth, not by an even ration across the scene.
S3 TESTABLE seed: Paragraph length varies deliberately across a passage. Uniform paragraphs are the strongest tell that nobody was making choices.
S4 TESTABLE seed: Sentence length varies within a paragraph. A short sentence after three long ones is a beat; three short ones in a row is a stutter.
S5 TESTABLE seed: Consecutive paragraphs do not all open on the same subject or pronoun.
S6 TESTABLE owner: A paragraph of dialogue carries at most three sentences of speech.
S7 TESTABLE owner: The emphasis sits on the speech itself and on how the conversation is going, not on the surrounding description.
S8 TESTABLE owner: A dialogue paragraph shows what the speakers are doing and what it would look like to someone standing there — write it as a third party would see and hear it.
S9 TESTABLE seed: Attribution stays plain. When the attribution verbs are doing the emotional work, the paragraph is labelling the conversation instead of showing it.
S10 TESTABLE seed: A scene reaches at least two senses. Sight alone is a slideshow.
S11 TESTABLE seed: Cut the filter between reader and moment — prefer what happened over the report of someone perceiving it happening.
S12 TESTABLE seed: Prefer the nameable thing to the abstraction wherever both would serve.
